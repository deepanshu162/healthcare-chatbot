About HealthAI v0.3

🗄️ Dual-Pane Clinical Notepad

The left pane provides interactive follow-up dialogue, while the right pane automatically compiles a structured Clinical Assessment Note.

☁️ MongoDB Cloud Persistence

HealthAI securely stores your consultations, follow-up records, and clinical notepad sheets in MongoDB Atlas.

👤 User Authentication

Create an account to securely access your symptom assessments and clinical notepad summaries from any device.

🛡️ Medical Disclaimer

HealthAI is not a doctor and does not provide medical diagnoses or prescriptions. It provides educational symptom assessment and encourages professional medical consultation.

🚨 Emergency Prioritization

If urgent symptoms are detected, HealthAI immediately flags emergency instructions rather than asking follow-up questions.
