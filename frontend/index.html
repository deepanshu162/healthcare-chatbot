🛡️ Safety Guidelines, Risk Assessment & Medical Disclaimer

👨‍⚕️ Educational Guidance Only

HealthAI is designed to provide educational information and help you understand symptoms and prescriptions. It is not a doctor and does not provide official medical diagnoses or prescriptions.

🚨 Medical Emergency Warning

If you have severe chest pain, acute shortness of breath, sudden numbness, high fever in infants, or severe trauma, call your local emergency services or seek immediate urgent medical care.

✍️ Unclear Handwriting Policy

Doctor handwriting can be difficult to read. If a medicine, dose, or test is unreadable, HealthAI will never guess. Unclear items are marked with instructions to verify with your doctor or pharmacist.

💊 Dosage & Medication Safety

Never alter prescribed dosages or stop/start medications based on AI output. Always consult your physician or pharmacist before taking medications or making treatment decisions.
